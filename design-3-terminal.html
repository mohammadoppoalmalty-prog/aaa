“The parts nobody photographs — error handling, migration paths, the second week after launch — decide whether software is trustworthy at 3 a.m.”

      
Six years of shipping software other people depend on: scheduling systems, internal tools, and the unglamorous layer underneath a product. I have owned things end to end and handed things over cleanly, which is a different and underrated skill.

      
This site exists because a bullet list never conveyed any of that. So I put the whole thing somewhere you can walk through, then made very sure you could skip the walk.
