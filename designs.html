How to judge these fairly
  • ◈Open each one and scroll all the way down. Half the design decisions live below the hero.
  • ◈Resize the window narrow. All five are responsive, and how they collapse tells you a lot.
  • ◈Hover the project cards, the buttons, and the headings — the motion differs sharply between directions.
  • ◈Ask one question of each: would a recruiter trust this in ninety seconds? That is the job.
  • ◈You can mix. Say “04's typography with 02's dark palette” and I will build that.
