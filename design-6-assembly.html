Design note · the synthesis, and why it is not a feature mashup

  • ◈One idea, not three. Aurora gave atmosphere, isometric gave dimension, the exploded boot gave ritual — but all three were secretly about a world being assembled. So the page has exactly one hero concept: a broken world rebuilt in front of you, in 3D, inside living air. Three hero moments competing is how merges fail.
  • ◈The city is the data. Every block's height is proportional to a real number — 6 years, 10 projects, 19 places, 16 puzzles, 100 memories, 5 endings. Hover any block and the readout below tells you what it is. A bar chart disguised as a toy, so the decoration earns its place instead of just occupying it.
  • ◈One light source, obeyed everywhere. Upper-left. Every block's top face is brightness(1.28), its left face .82, its right face .58 — never randomised. Every glass panel catches the same light on its top edge, weighted left, and down its left border. This single rule is most of why it reads as real rather than decorated.
  • ◈A motion budget, spent once per screen height. Boot assembles · hero tilts · cards lift · bars fill · CTA breathes. Never two hero motions at the same time. Restraint is the reason it feels expensive instead of busy.
  • ◈The cyclist was re-cast, not pasted in. He now rides on the isometric plate, rim-lit in the world's cyan — which makes him a scale figure, the classic architectural-rendering trick for making a model read as a place. Same joy, but he now serves the composition. Click him. Press B to send him home; the choice is remembered.
  • ◈The air is alive but cheap. The aurora is a half-resolution canvas of six additive blobs under a 74 px blur — a few hundred bytes of state, one draw per frame, and it pauses the moment the tab is hidden. A cursor-tracked light layer makes the space feel physical for the cost of one transform.
  • ◈Replace before shipping: the portrait, the quote and two bio paragraphs, the email and social handles, the skill percentages, and all three project cards. The shapes are correct; the facts are invented.
