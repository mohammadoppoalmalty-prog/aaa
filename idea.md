# PROJECT NAME

"The Forgotten Mind"

Subtitle:

A Cinematic Interactive Portfolio Experience

=========================================================
MISSION
=========================================================

Create one of the most unique portfolio websites ever made.

This should not look like a website.

It should not feel like a website.

It should feel like an award-winning indie exploration game with cinematic storytelling, environmental puzzles, dynamic events, emotional atmosphere, AI interactions, and hidden secrets.

The visitor should completely forget they are inside a browser.

Instead...

They become the main character.

Their objective is to restore a forgotten world by recovering the memories of the developer.

Every discovered memory reveals another piece of the developer's personality, journey, projects, skills and dreams.

=========================================================
LEGACY SYSTEM
=========================================================

The adventure should never truly end.

When the player restores the world and reaches the final portal, they are offered one final choice:

"Leave a memory behind."

Instead of showing a traditional contact form immediately, the player enters a peaceful sanctuary.

At the center stands an enormous glowing tree known as:

The Tree of Visitors.

This tree represents everyone who has ever explored the world.

The player can:

• Leave a short message.
• Leave their name or a nickname.
• Choose a glowing leaf color or magical symbol.
• Optionally attach a small emoji or icon.

When submitted:

A new glowing leaf grows on the tree.

The tree gently reacts.

Wind flows through its branches.

Soft music plays.

The Guardian smiles.

Future visitors can discover these leaves, read approved messages, and realize that every explorer has left part of themselves behind.

The tree continues growing over time, making the world feel alive and evolving.
